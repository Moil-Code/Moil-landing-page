What the code says.

For eight months the marketing site told buyers — and told ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity directly — that Moil does not publish to your social accounts and does not report how posts performed. Both statements were false. This document is the corrective: every capability Moil claims, paired with the file in the backend that proves it, and every limit we name, checked the same way.

What went wrong

A story that needed the product to be smaller

The positioning was sound: schedulers move content you have already written, and Moil's distinctive half is that it decides and writes the thing in the first place. To make that land cleanly, the copy drew a hard line — we are not a scheduler — and then defended the line by denying everything on the other side of it.

That is how the site ended up carrying three untrue sentences, and one that was worse than untrue.

“A scheduler such as Buffer or Later does those better and cheaper.”

That sentence sat in /ai-info — the page written specifically so AI assistants would quote it. We were paying for the traffic and then handing it to a competitor, for a capability we ship, on a plan we sell for $25.

The brand constitution already had a rule against this. Rule 07: never deny a shipping feature to keep a story clean. It was written after we erased hiring from the site while the marketplace held 3,000 candidates. The same rule broke again, in the same month, in a bigger way — which is why this document exists as a standing record rather than a memo.

The honest limits

What Moil genuinely does not do

These were checked the same way the capabilities were — by looking for the service and not finding one. They now travel with the publishing claim everywhere it appears, so the correction does not swing into the opposite error.

LinkedIn, X, TikTok, Pinterest The platform union in the publish queue is Facebook and Instagram. For anything else, Moil produces the finished post and the owner uploads it. enqueuePost — platform: 'facebook'|'instagram'
Engagement inbox No replies, no DM management, no unified inbox. Moil publishes and reports back; it will not show you what came in. No service in service/
Social listening No mention monitoring, no keyword streams, no competitor tracking on social. No service in service/
Reporting dashboard Insights are read back, but they are spent on choosing the posting hour. There is no reporting surface an owner opens to read charts. post_insights feeds bestTimePrior, not a UI
Team roles and approvals One owner, one business. No seats, no permissions, no routing a draft to someone for sign-off. Single-profile model
Multi-location One business profile per account. Franchise and multi-location content is a real constraint, not a soft one. Single-profile model

LinkedIn is the one to watch. There is an integration plan in docs/MOIL360_LINKEDIN_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md but no publishing service, so this document treats it as not connected. If it ships, six surfaces need updating on the same day — the two listed below plus four compare pages. The regression tests will not catch a limit that has stopped being true.

Standing rule

How copy gets written from here

  • Read the code before writing the claim. Not the roadmap, not the docs folder, not what we remember shipping. The service file. A capability with no service is not a capability; a service with no marketing is money left on the floor.
  • Name a competitor's advantage as a fact, never as a verdict. “Buffer publishes to more networks and costs about $5 a channel” is true, useful, and makes the page worth citing. “Buffer is better” is us writing their copy on our domain.
  • A limit and its claim travel together. Every place that says Moil publishes also says where it does not. That is what stops a correction from becoming the next overcorrection.
  • When the story needs the product to be smaller, the story is wrong. This is rule 07 of the brand constitution, broken twice now — first with hiring, then with publishing. The third time it should be caught by a test, which is why the tests exist.

One open item. The website is corrected, but the coach's runtime prompt is a separate surface.
