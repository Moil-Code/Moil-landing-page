# EDC partner page research

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Scope:** Partner-card facts for Queen Creek Economic Development and Buda Economic Development Corporation. Claims below are supported only by the organizations' official government or organization websites and official planning/budget documents.

## Page-ready cards

### Town of Queen Creek Economic Development

- **Official name to display:** Town of Queen Creek Economic Development
- **Location:** Queen Creek, Arizona — part of the Phoenix East Valley
- **Suggested card copy:** Supporting sustainable growth in Queen Creek through business attraction, expansion, private investment, and targeted-industry development. The Town's economic-development strategy focuses on advanced manufacturing and electrification, logistics and industrial services, energy and infrastructure services, digital/IT/business operations, and agriculture and destination economy.
- **Partner link:** <https://investtheqc.com/>

**Source support:** The Town of Queen Creek's adopted budget describes the Economic Development Division as responsible for business attraction, retention and expansion, plus promoting private-investment opportunities. The Town's Economic Development Strategic Plan (adopted May 2026) lists the five clusters and says the plan guides economic-development decisions, policies, marketing and recruitment. [Town budget, Economic Development section](https://www.queencreekaz.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/39701/638590490091500000) · [Economic Development Strategic Plan](https://www.queencreekaz.gov/departments/economic-development/strategic-plan) · [Town business resources](https://www.queencreekaz.gov/business)

**Naming note:** Official Town pages present this as the Town of Queen Creek's Economic Development Department/Division, with an advisory Economic Development Commission; they do not identify a standalone entity formally named “Queen Creek EDC.” Use “Town of Queen Creek Economic Development” rather than expanding “EDC” into a corporate name.

### Buda Economic Development Corporation (Buda EDC)

- **Official name to display:** Buda Economic Development Corporation (Buda EDC)
- **Location:** Buda, Texas
- **Suggested card copy:** Buda EDC is the City of Buda's primary economic-development organization. It works to create balanced growth by recruiting and retaining diverse businesses, developing infrastructure, supporting local businesses and entrepreneurs, and protecting the region's quality of life and natural resources.
- **Partner link:** <https://www.budaedc.com/>

**Source support:** Buda EDC's FY 2025–26 budget narrative identifies it as the City of Buda's primary economic-development organization and states its mission. The narrative also names business recruitment and retention, small-business and entrepreneur support, infrastructure investment, workforce development, and destination marketing as key activities. [Buda EDC FY 2025–26 Budget Narrative, pp. 2–3](https://www.budaedc.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/1.-Final-Council-Approved-FY26-BEDC-Budget-Narrative.pdf) · [Buda EDC site-selection resources](https://www.budaedc.com/site-selection/)
