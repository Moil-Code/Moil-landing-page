# Business story sections redesign

## Decision

Use an **editorial operating system** across the four mid-page story sections. The system shares a numbered section rail, one exact action color (`#FF6633`), fine rules, quiet surfaces, asymmetric grids, and product evidence. Each section then uses a structure matched to its job:

1. **Your AI Co-Founder — Manifesto workbench.** The positioning statement and founder tension sit above two labeled product outputs. This turns the promise into evidence without falling back to a generic feature card.
2. **Platform Capabilities — Capability registry.** Three compact capability records create scan speed, while Moil360 becomes the flagship instrument and document creation closes the stack.
3. **Your Complete Journey — Operational timeline.** A semantic ordered sequence sits beside the live coach interface. The persistent visual makes the flow feel like a working system, not an infographic.
4. **Built for the America That Exists — Bilingual comparison lab.** EN/ES is demonstrated through the draggable product comparison, then supported by a three-part proof ledger.

## Directions considered

| Direction | Strength | Limitation | Score |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Editorial operating system | Strong hierarchy, product proof, brand restraint, high scanability | Requires tailored layouts per section | **94/100** |
| Unified dashboard shell | Immediately reads as software | Makes the landing page feel like an admin product and flattens the story | 81/100 |
| Cinematic scroll narrative | Memorable and unconventional | Motion-heavy, slower to scan, weaker accessibility and mobile resilience | 76/100 |

The editorial direction was selected because it creates coherence through rules and rhythm while preserving a distinct content model for each section.

## Brand contract

- Orange-filled controls, numbered nodes, and icon tiles use `#FF6633` with white text or icons.
- Orange is the only primary action color. Purple is no longer used as a competing emphasis in these sections.
- Surface contrast, 1px rules, and typography establish hierarchy before shadows or decoration.
- Product imagery is framed as evidence and labeled with lightweight index rails.

## Responsive contract

- Desktop: asymmetric two-column compositions preserve editorial tension.
- Tablet: headers reduce to a numbered rail plus one content column; capability and journey bodies stack where necessary.
- Mobile: all sections become one column, controls remain at least 50px high, labels wrap, and the comparison rail is removed before it can compress the product image.
